Implemented:
- Full and incremental game-state reconstruction, with checksums and diff bounds.
- Recursive typed attributes, including large integers and string references.
- Legacy turn-step prompts and typed interaction-button descriptors.
- Available per-card actions and live in-game object identifiers.
- NewCardActionMessage and PromptActionMessage submission through the game route.
- A small policy for land drops, affordable untargeted spells, mana sources,
  confirmations, response passes, and turn advancement.
- Current state observations including life, hand/library/graveyard counts.
- Duplicate-submission suppression keyed by timestamp AND reconstructed state.

Two useful protocol details:
1. Payment can change state while keeping the interaction timestamp unchanged.
   Timestamp-only deduplication would stop the bot after its first mana source.
2. Ordinary legacy priority prompts can have an unused trailing zero byte.
   The official decoder ignores it; our parser now accepts that zero padding.

Card metadata stayed local:
The installed client's CardDataSource XML supplied names, mana costs, card types,
and text. Its embedded StrConst_v3.dat supplied protocol action strings.
An attempted Scryfall lookup was rejected by automatic approval review because
it would send private saved-deck card identifiers to an external service. No
lookup was sent; the local-data route completed the task.
Local protocol strings and card metadata are cached in the Git-ignored .local/
directory; no proprietary asset bundle or credentials are included in Git.

Validation: 17 offline tests pass, covering framing, credentials, envelopes,
match/solitaire layouts, state checksums/diffs, attribute/string decoding, prompt
padding, action encoding, and basic policy decisions. Live evidence includes
resolved permanents, spell graveyard entries, token creation, life changes,
priority/turn progression, and terminal server messages.

Limitations:
- The policy avoids direct burn spells requiring targets; in SOLO their available
  player target is ourselves. It handles untargeted Flame Rift/Vortex normally.
- It does not implement strategic attacking, blocking, modal spells, complex
  target requirements, ordering, or all numerical/selection prompts.
- Unknown prompts pause the policy. Unsupported state decoding pauses decisions
  while keeping the backend connection alive.
- Tests and this playthrough demonstrate a limited solitaire path, not complete
  coverage of MTG gameplay or competitive Legacy Burn strategy.
- Source and notes are saved locally; this stage has not been committed/pushed.
